# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Data Analysis (Excel Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the **Vrinda Store Annual Sales Data (2022)** using **Microsoft Excel**. The main objective was to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, top-performing states, and profitable sales channels through data analysis and dashboard visualization.

The project includes **data cleaning, pivot tables, charts, interactive dashboard creation, and business insights extraction** to support better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objective
The goal of this project was to:

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Identify top-performing states
- Compare men vs women buying trends
- Analyze order status and channel contribution
- Generate business recommendations to improve sales

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Women Customers Dominated Sales
Women contributed approximately **64–65%** of total purchases compared to men.

### 2. Top Performing States
The highest sales came from:

- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Uttar Pradesh

### 3. High-Contributing Age Group
The **Adult age group (30–49 years)** contributed the maximum share of total sales.

### 4. Best Sales Channels
The top-performing channels were:

- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Myntra

---

## 💡 Final Business Recommendation
To improve Vrinda Store sales:

**Target women customers aged 30–49 years living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh by offering ads, discounts, coupons, and promotional campaigns on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.**
